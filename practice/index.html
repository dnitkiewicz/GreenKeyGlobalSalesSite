The program in plain terms

What certification actually involves

The Eco-Rating is an assessment of how a property is operated. 213 questions across five areas: corporate management, housekeeping, engineering, food and beverage, and conference and meeting services. Answers are supported by evidence uploaded from the property.

An independent inspection firm audits the submission and an independent certification body issues the rating. Properties are rated 1 to 5 Keys, and certification is valid for three years.

Conference and Food & Beverage are optional. A property with no meeting space is not scored against one that has it, and the score rescales without penalty.

The path

From first login to a certificate on the wall

  1. Assess

    Online self-assessment with multiple users and progress saved as you go.

    About 4 to 6 hours of focused work

  2. Evaluate

    Preliminary rating and a gap scorecard. Retake freely before you request the audit.

    Immediate

  3. Audit

    Independent verification, then a certification decision by an independent certification body.

    Commences within about 90 days of request

  4. Certify

    A rating of 1 to 5 Keys, plus a Property Performance Report showing where the next Key sits.

    Valid three years

  5. Market

    Marketing toolkit, OTA badges, RFP content, and distribution of your property listing.

    Ongoing

What it does for the business

The assessment is not asking a hotel to spend money on sustainability. It is asking it to stop spending money on waste.

The practices the assessment rewards are the same practices that cut spend. Metering utilities. Preventative maintenance. Sealing the building envelope. Lighting upgrades. Water-conscious housekeeping routines. Right-sized food purchasing. None of that is a sustainability program. It is operating discipline that happens to score.
